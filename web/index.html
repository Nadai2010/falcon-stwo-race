STARK proofs are hash-based and already post-quantum. Signatures were the weak point. Three races, original against record, in the same configuration: a full proof at 100 provable bits, a proof in about a gigabyte of memory, and a Falcon-512 signature checked by a Starknet account.

Press R to start, Esc to stop, F to fit the screen.

Built together

Every record stands on earlier work.

These are the people whose public activity on provably.fast fed the records raced above: leads, builds, reproductions, negative results and reviews. The trees include all of it; "record" means the community's best, not one person's.

    An independent community demo. Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, StarkWare, the Starknet Foundation or OpenZeppelin; their names and logos belong to them and only say what the races run on.

    Original and record always run the same statement, security parameters and thread budget. Proofs are checked by the season's verifier; gas is Sierra gas reported by Starknet Foundry. Official figures are labelled with their source and date.
